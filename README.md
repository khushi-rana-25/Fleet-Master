# 🚛 Fleet Management System

Welcome to the **Fleet Management System**, a powerful and elegant iOS application built to streamline the operations of fleets, drivers, and trips in real-time. Designed for transport companies, logistics managers, or any business with vehicles on the move—this app is your control tower. 🛰️

## 🔥 Why This App?

Managing a fleet isn’t just about vehicles—it's about coordination, real-time updates, safety, cost-efficiency, and above all, control. This system gives the **fleet manager** dedicated tools to keep everything running like a dream.

---

## 🧠 Core Features

### ✅ For Fleet Manager
- 📍 **Real-time Tracking** – Monitor active trips on a live map with vehicle status and ETA.
- 🚦 **KPI Dashboard** – View total vehicles, trips, pending maintenance, and alerts at a glance.
- ⚠️ **Alerts & Notifications** – Instantly see breakdowns, violations, or maintenance issues.
- 💡 **Quick Actions** – Start trips, assign drivers, or schedule maintenance with ease and accuracy.
